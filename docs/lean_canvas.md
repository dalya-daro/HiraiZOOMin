- **Name:** Dalya (myself)

## ① Whose

**Who will use this app?**

Foreign visitors and newcomers staying in or visiting Hiraizumi who are unfamiliar with the area, especially people who prefer to receive information in English.

- **Initial user:** Dalya, a foreigner staying in Hiraizumi.
- **First testers:** Sparta coding campmates and foreign friends.

## ② What kind of problems

1. Visitors do not know which tourist attractions and interesting places to explore.
2. Visitors do not know which local restaurants or food spots to try.
3. Newcomers do not know where to find supermarkets and shops for daily necessities.
4. Searching on maps or websites can make it difficult to discover local places and decide where to go.
5. Language barriers can make it difficult to ask local people for recommendations.

**Current alternative methods:**

- Searching on Google or Google Maps.
- Asking local residents or friends for recommendations.
- Checking tourist brochures, paper maps, or information boards.
- Searching for information on social media or tourism websites.

## ③ In a word

HiraiZOOMin is an English-friendly local discovery web app that helps people unfamiliar with Hiraizumi find places to visit, local food to try, and shops for daily necessities in one place.

## ④ What you can do with the app (3 things to show on Day 8)

1. Browse a list of local places, restaurants, and shops in Hiraizumi.
2. Filter places by category, such as sightseeing, food, and shopping.
3. View the details of a selected place, including its name, description, address, and other available information.

## ⑤ Why I make it

After spending time in Hiraizumi, I realized that the town has many interesting places and good local food that foreign visitors might miss because they do not know where to find them. Language barriers can also make it difficult to ask local residents for recommendations.

I want to create an easy-to-use web app that helps visitors discover more of Hiraizumi independently and makes local information easier to access.

## Recommended App Structure

The first version will have a simple structure that can be developed using Python, Flask, and PeeWee.

1. **Home Page:** Introduce HiraiZOOMin and display the main categories.
2. **Category Selection:** Let users choose sightseeing, food, or shopping.
3. **Place List:** Display places belonging to the selected category.
4. **Place Details:** Show the selected place's name, description, address, and an optional map link.

**Recommended development scope:**

- Use Python for application logic and place information processing.
- Use Flask to create web pages and handle category filtering.
- Use PeeWee to store and retrieve place information from a database, if required by the camp.
- Start with a small number of places and expand after the main features work.
- Do not include GPS navigation, user accounts, live maps, or complicated recommendation systems in the first version.
